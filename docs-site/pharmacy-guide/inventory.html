Product Catalogue & Store Inventory

Pulse separates what a product is (the catalogue) from how much of it you have (inventory and stock records).

Product Catalogue​

The catalogue is the shared master list of products across the Pulse network. Every pharmacy draws from the same catalogue, which keeps names, strengths, and SKUs consistent.

Catalogue fields​

FieldPurpose
Product name, generic name, brand nameIdentify the medicine
Strength & dosage forme.g. 500mg capsule
Pack sizeUnits per pack
SKUYour stock-keeping code
CategoryGrouping for browsing and filters
Cost & selling priceIn ZMK
QuantityTarget on-hand stock to maintain
Min stock levelAlert floor
Reorder levelReplenishment trigger

Adding or editing a product​

  1. Open Inventory → Product Catalogue.
  2. Click Add Product (or the edit icon on a row).
  3. Fill the form — Product Name and SKU are required.
  4. The margin indicator under the pricing fields shows your mark-up as you type.
  5. Save. Products with images show a photo tile in the grid view.

Search, filter, and view​

  • Type in the search box to match name, generic, brand, or SKU.
  • Filter by category chips.
  • Toggle between grid (photo cards) and list (dense table) views.

Store Inventory​

Store Inventory is the pharmacy-side stock list: every item you carry with its live quantity, price, batch, and status. Use it to:

  • See what's physically on the shelf right now.
  • Spot low stock at a glance (red quantity + Low Stock badge).
  • Open an item for its full detail: movements, batches, and sales history.
Catalogue vs inventory in one sentence

The catalogue says "this is Amoxicillin 500mg, it costs K45" — inventory says "this pharmacy currently has 240 of them, in batches B-101 and B-105".

Importing products in bulk​

Use the Import Wizard to load products from a spreadsheet. See Import Wizard for templates, column mappings, and reconciliation behaviour.
