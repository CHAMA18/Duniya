What is AURA?

Banks lose $487 billion every year to fraud and bad loans. The reason? Two separate teams — credit and fraud — never talk to each other. AURA fixes this by combining both into one intelligent system.

🔴 The Problem

Credit Team asks: "Can they repay?"

Uses old-fashioned models. Checks income and collateral. Updates once every few months.

Fraud Team asks: "Will they repay?"

Uses fast AI. Watches every transaction. Updates in milliseconds.

⚡ THE GAP — Where these two teams meet is where $487B in losses disappear every year

💡 The Big Idea

Credit default and fraud are actually the same thing — just seen through different time windows. A loan that defaults after 18 months? That's slow-motion fraud. A stolen card used for 30 seconds? That's fast-motion credit loss. AURA watches both windows at once.

🟢 The Solution — 4 Simple Pieces
1

One Brain

One AI model that understands both credit risk AND fraud — instead of two separate models that never share notes.

2

Your Digital Twin

A "doppelgänger" for each customer that predicts their normal behaviour. When reality diverges — alert.

3

Time Telescopes

Looks at the same person through two time windows: seconds (fraud) and months (credit). Catches what neither team sees alone.

4

Self-Attacking System

AI agents constantly attack the system in shadow mode — like a immune system training itself against new threats.
