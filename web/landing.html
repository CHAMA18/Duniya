Features

Everything your pharmacy needs

From inventory tracking to point-of-sale, Duniya gives you complete control over every aspect of your pharmacy operations.

Smart Inventory

Real-time stock tracking with automated low-stock alerts, batch management, and expiry monitoring. Never run out of critical medications again.

Point of Sale

Fast, intuitive checkout with barcode scanning, prescription tracking, and multi-payment support. Built for the pace of pharmacy retail.

Analytics & Reports

Comprehensive dashboards with sales trends, revenue analytics, and inventory insights. Make data-driven decisions with confidence.

VMI & Supply Chain

Vendor-managed inventory with automated replenishment, stock movements, goods received tracking, and outlet-level visibility.

HR & Team Management

Role-based access control, staff management, performance tracking, and approval workflows. Keep your team organized and accountable.

Offline-First

Full functionality without internet. Cache-warming, offline sync, and connectivity-aware UI ensure zero downtime, even in low-connectivity areas.

Security

Enterprise-grade security by default

Your pharmacy data is protected with the same security standards used by leading healthcare organizations worldwide.

End-to-End Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Your pharmacy records, patient data, and financial information remain fully protected at every stage.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permissions with 8 specialized roles ensure every team member sees only what they need. Owners, pharmacists, technicians, and assistants each have tailored access levels.

Firebase Authentication

Secure authentication powered by Google Firebase with support for email/password, Google Sign-In, and Apple Sign-In. Multi-factor authentication and session management built in.

Real-Time Audit Trail

Every action is logged with timestamps and user attribution. Full audit trails for sales, inventory changes, and staff activities ensure complete accountability and compliance.
